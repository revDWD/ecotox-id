    About

    Ecotox ID is a free, ad-free hobby project adapted from Wild ID. The ecotoxicology notes are brief, general educational remarks and are not regulatory or risk-assessment advice; check primary sources and test guidelines before relying on them. The battle layout is an homage to classic monster-collecting RPGs; all art here is original and the game is not affiliated with any game company.
